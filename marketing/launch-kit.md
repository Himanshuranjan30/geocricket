## 2. r/SideProject — today
**Title:** I built a daily cricket geography game: GeoGuessr meets Wordle for cricket fans
**Body:**
Cricket has a billion fans and every "cricket Wordle" asks you to guess a player. I wanted the *where*: where did Lara score 400, where did Dhoni hit the 2011 six.

GeoCricket gives you 5 famous moments a day; you pin each on a 3D globe and score by distance. Things I built that I'm proud of:
- One shared Daily for everyone (Wordle-style), plus Test Matches at 8 AM and 8 PM
- Spoiler-free WhatsApp result card
- Ghost Race: an instant 1v1 against a real player's recorded run
- Weekly leagues and an ICC-style ranking

Stack: Next.js, MapLibre globe, Postgres. Launched today, solo. Brutal feedback welcome: https://geocricket.app
